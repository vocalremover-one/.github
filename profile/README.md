# VocalRemover.one

[VocalRemover.one](https://vocalremover.one/) separates a mixed recording into a vocal track and an instrumental. To hear what that sounds like before uploading anything, compare the **original**, **vocals**, and **instrumental** in the demo on the homepage. Follow the end of the same sung phrase in all three versions. You can hear which track keeps the singer’s reverb after the voice stops.

## Which track do you need?

| What you’re working on | Where to start | What to listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A backing track for rehearsal | [Instrumental Maker](https://vocalremover.one/instrumental-maker) | Sing over the preview at rehearsal volume. A faint harmony may blend with your voice, while a clearly audible lead can make it hard to practice your own phrasing. |
| A vocal for transcription or a remix | [Acapella Extractor](https://vocalremover.one/acapella-extractor) | For transcription, check that you can hear consonants and where each phrase ends. For a remix, listen in the gaps between lines for cymbals or chords that could clash with the new beat. |
| A drum part or bass line to learn by ear | [Stem Splitter](https://vocalremover.one/stem-splitter) | Choose the four-track split for vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments. Compare the bass and drum tracks at the same timestamp to hear how the bass notes line up with the kick. |

## Getting a representative preview

Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A, or FLAC file. If you have several copies of the recording, start with the original WAV or FLAC, or the highest-quality MP3 you have. Converting a low-bitrate MP3 to WAV preserves the compression artifacts already in the recording.

The free preview covers **up to the first 60 seconds** and is available for playback. If the song opens with a long instrumental intro, that preview tells you little about vocal removal. To test a difficult chorus before buying, make a short excerpt from your source file with the chorus at the start and upload it for a preview. Use the complete original when you’re ready to process the whole song.

## Downloads

For a song longer than the preview, choose full-song processing from the result page. You can review the paid options there. Once the complete track is ready, choose the stem you need. MP3 is convenient for a rehearsal playlist. WAV avoids another lossy encoding step when you bring the stem into an audio editor.

For help with a result, email [support@vocalremover.one](mailto:support@vocalremover.one). Include the tool you used, the stem you were listening to, and a timestamp where the issue is easiest to hear.
