# VocalRemover.one

[VocalRemover.one](https://vocalremover.one/) separates a song into two tracks in your browser: isolated vocals and an instrumental backing track. Use the instrumental for singing practice, or listen to the vocals on their own to learn a harmony.

## Separate your first song

1. Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A, or FLAC file.
2. Play the same passage in both tracks. A chorus is a good place to check how backing vocals and layered instruments sound after separation.
3. Preview the full result for free. If you want to keep the tracks, unlock the downloads.
